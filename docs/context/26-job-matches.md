# Job matches and recommendations

## 1. What this is

**Job matches** shows you roles from the platform ranked by how well they fit your profile. Each
match carries a fit score and a rank, along with the role's company, department, location,
employment type, experience level, salary range and application deadline.

Separately, the public **Careers** pages list AlgoJob's own open roles — that is a normal job board,
not a personalised match list.

## 2. Who can use it

Every candidate. Matches appear once there are roles on the platform that fit your profile.

## 3. Names and terms

| A user might say | Meaning |
|---|---|
| matches, my matches | the ranked job recommendations |
| match score, fit | how well a role fits your profile |
| marketplace | a page that is **not currently functional** — see section 6 |
| careers | AlgoJob's own job openings, a public page |

## 4. How it works

- Matches are generated for you from your profile — your skills, experience and preferences.
- Each match carries a **score** and a **rank**, so the list is ordered by fit.
- Open a match to see the full role details: company, department, location, employment type,
  experience level, salary range, notice period, whether remote work is allowed, and the
  application deadline.
- You can sort and filter the list.

**Matches are refreshed for you rather than generated on demand.** A brand-new or sparse profile
will have few matches or none until there are roles that fit it.

## 5. When things go wrong

### "I have no job matches"
The usual reasons, in order:
1. **Your profile is incomplete.** Matching works from your skills and experience — fill in your
   profile and upload your resume. See `20-accounts-and-login.md`.
2. **There are no roles that fit you yet.** Matches only appear when a suitable role exists on the
   platform.
3. **Your matches have not refreshed yet.** The list updates periodically, not the instant you edit
   your profile.

### "My matches are irrelevant"
Matching reads your profile. If your resume is outdated or your listed skills do not reflect what
you actually do, correct your profile — that is the input.

### "A match disappeared"
Roles close and deadlines pass. A role that is no longer accepting applications drops out.

### "The match score seems wrong"
The score reflects fit against the role's requirements, not your overall ability. A strong candidate
can score low on a poorly-fitting role, and that is the score working correctly.

## 6. The marketplace page is not a working feature

There is a **marketplace** page showing badges, avatars and boosts with prices in points. **It is
not functional.** Nothing can be bought there, and XP cannot be spent on anything.

If a user asks about it, say the marketplace is not currently available and that XP is not a
spendable currency — it converts automatically into interview credits and moves your level. See
`22-xp-levels-and-rewards.md`. **Never tell a user they can buy anything with XP.**

## 7. Common questions

**How do I get more matches?**
Complete your profile and keep your resume current. That is the input matching works from.

**Can I apply to a match through AlgoJob?**
Open the role to see its details and how to proceed. Where an application route is not offered in
the product, route the question to support rather than guessing.

**Do recruiters see that I was matched to their role?**
Matching produces your list. It does not by itself notify a recruiter.

**Is the match score shown to recruiters?**
Treat the score as yours. Do not tell a user recruiters rank them by it.

**What is the difference between Matches and Careers?**
Matches are roles on the platform ranked for you. Careers is AlgoJob's own hiring page.

**Can I spend my XP on badges?**
No. The marketplace page is not functional and XP cannot be spent.

## 8. What this does not do

- It does not let you buy anything with XP.
- It does not generate matches on demand the moment you edit your profile.
- It does not guarantee a role exists that fits you.
- It does not apply to roles on your behalf.

## 9. When to contact support

- No matches at all despite a complete profile and a current resume.
- A role that will not open or shows incomplete details.
- Any question about applying to a specific role that the product does not answer directly.
