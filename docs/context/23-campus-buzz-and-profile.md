# Campus Buzz, your profile, analytics and referrals

## 1. What this is

**Campus Buzz** is the social side of AlgoJob — a feed where students post, follow each other and
see achievements. **Analytics** is your personal performance dashboard. **Your public profile** is
what other students see. **Referrals** let you share a code with friends.

## 2. Who can use it

Every candidate.

## 3. Names and terms

| A user might say | Meaning |
|---|---|
| the feed, buzz, timeline | **Campus Buzz** |
| auto post | a post the platform created for you after an achievement |
| share link | the public link to a single post |
| radar, spider chart | the skill chart in Analytics |
| refer, invite | the referral code |

## 4. Campus Buzz

- **Post** text and images to the feed.
- **Like** and **follow** other students.
- **Automatic posts** are created for you on notable achievements — levelling up, and completing an
  assessment with a strong score. Only scores of **75% or above** generate an automatic post, so a
  weak result is never broadcast.
- **Share a post publicly.** Each post has a public link that works without signing in, so you can
  share an achievement outside the platform.

## 5. Your profile and analytics

**Your public profile** shows who you are and your achievements. Other students can view it.

**Analytics** is private to you and covers:
- Performance over time across every arena.
- A **skill radar** showing your relative strength by skill.
- **Heatmaps** of your activity and topic coverage.
- Your standing on individual skills.

**Report** is separate — it holds your per-attempt reports from every product. Analytics is the
aggregate view; Report is the per-attempt detail.

## 6. Referrals

You get a referral code you can share.

⚠️ **Referral rewards are not currently being credited.** The code exists and can be shared, but do
not promise a user any reward, XP, credit or discount for referring someone. If a user asks what
they get, say the referral programme is not currently active and offer to pass the question to
support.

## 7. When things go wrong

### "My achievement was not posted"
Automatic posts are only created for scores of 75% or above, and for level-ups. Below that
threshold nothing is posted — deliberately, so a weak attempt is never broadcast.

### "I do not want my results posted publicly"
Automatic posts go to Campus Buzz. Delete any post you do not want visible.

### "My share link does not work for my friend"
Public post links work without signing in. If the post has been deleted the link stops working.

### "My analytics look empty"
Analytics builds from completed, **scored** attempts. An attempt still being scored has not landed
yet. Complete at least one attempt in an arena before expecting a chart for it.

### "My skill radar does not match my reports"
The radar aggregates across attempts and weights recent performance. A single strong report does not
move it immediately.

### "I referred a friend and got nothing"
The referral programme is not currently crediting rewards. Apologise for the confusion and route to
support.

### "I appear on the wrong college leaderboard"
Fix your college in your profile. See `20-accounts-and-login.md`.

## 8. Common questions

**Can I make my profile private?**
Profile visibility options are not documented — route to support rather than guessing.

**Can I delete a post?**
Yes, your own posts.

**Do my practice results get posted automatically?**
Only strong ones — 75% and above — and level-ups.

**Can recruiters see my Campus Buzz posts?**
Posts are visible on the platform and any post you share with a public link is visible to anyone
with the link.

**What do I get for referring someone?**
The referral programme is not currently active. Do not promise a reward.

**What is the Blue Tick on the PRO plan?**
It is listed on the plan card but **is not currently available**. Do not promise it.

**Why is my analytics different from my report?**
Report is per-attempt. Analytics is the aggregate across attempts.

## 9. What this does not do

- Referral rewards are not currently credited.
- The Blue Tick advertised for Campus Buzz is not available.
- **AlgoBuddy / counselling is not currently available** — the menu item is hidden and it cannot
  be purchased.
- There is no direct messaging between students.

## 10. When to contact support

- Anything about referral rewards.
- A post that will not delete.
- Analytics still empty after several completed and scored attempts.
- Requests about profile privacy.
