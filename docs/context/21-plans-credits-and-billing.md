# Plans, credits and billing

The highest-traffic support topic. Be precise about numbers, and never promise a refund — see
section 11.

## 1. What this is

AlgoJob has two plans: **Basic**, which is free, and **PRO**, which is paid. Separately from plans,
you can buy **top-up packs** that add interview or assessment sessions to your account without
subscribing.

## 2. The single most important thing to get right

**PRO does not unlock products. It raises your limits.**

Every arena — Mock SVAR, Mock Debug, Mock Aptitude, Code Byte — and every basic report is available
on the free Basic plan. PRO gives you more attempts per week, more daily XP, advanced reports, a
monthly interview credit and cheaper top-ups.

If someone asks "do I need to pay to use SVAR / Debug / Aptitude?", the answer is **no**.

## 6. How interview credits are used

You can hold credits from three sources at once. They are spent in a fixed order, oldest-expiring
logic first:

1. **Your monthly PRO allowance** — use it or lose it at the end of the month.
2. **XP-earned credits** — every 300 XP earns one, valid 3 months.
3. **Purchased top-ups** — used in the order you bought them, each with its own expiry.

This ordering means your monthly allowance is always spent before credits you paid for.

**Assessment credits** (from the bundle packs) work differently: they are only consumed **once you
have hit your free weekly limit**. Practising within your weekly allowance never touches them.

## 7. Billing, renewal and cancellation

- **Payment is through Razorpay.** Cards, UPI and netbanking are handled there, not by AlgoJob.
- **You have 15 minutes to complete a checkout** before the order expires. An expired order is
  cleaned up automatically; start again.
- **Renewal is automatic** unless you cancel. A renewal order is created ahead of the renewal date
  and you have a few days to pay it, with a grace period on your access.
- **Upgrading** is immediate and prorated — you pay the difference and get PRO straight away.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at your next renewal date, not immediately. You keep PRO until then.
- **Cancelling** stops auto-renewal. The confirmation reads, exactly:
  *"Subscription cancelled. Access continues until the current billing period ends."*
  You keep PRO until the end of the period you already paid for.
- **You cannot pause a subscription.** Only cancel.
- **Invoices** are downloadable as PDFs from the Payments page.

## 8. When things go wrong

### "I paid but nothing happened"
Payment confirmation arrives from the payment provider, and there can be a short delay. Refresh the
Payments page. If the plan or credits have not appeared after a few minutes, **contact support with
your payment reference** — do not pay again.

### "I was charged twice"
Contact support with both payment references. Do not attempt a chargeback first — it is faster to
resolve directly.

### "My credit did not come back after a failed interview"
Credits are held at the start of an interview and only actually spent once a report exists. If no
report was produced, the hold releases automatically — allow about an hour. If it has not returned
after that, contact support.

### "I bought a top-up and my credits expired"
Top-up validity runs from purchase and is per pack — 1, 2 or 3 months for interview packs. Expired
credits are not restored. This is why the validity is shown on the pack before you buy.

### "I cancelled but I still have PRO"
Correct and intended. Cancelling stops the next charge; your access continues until the end of the
period you already paid for.

### "I cancelled by mistake"
Resubscribe from the Payments page. If your current period has not ended yet, contact support before
paying again.

### "I upgraded but was charged the full price"
Upgrades are prorated against your remaining period. If the amount looks wrong, contact support with
the order reference.

### "My checkout window closed"
Orders expire after 15 minutes. Start a new checkout.

### "I ran out of attempts but I am on PRO"
PRO gives 4 attempts per week per arena, not unlimited. Wait for the week to roll over or buy an
assessment bundle.

### "Where is PRO+?"
PRO+ is not available. There are two plans: Basic and PRO.

## 9. Common questions

**How much is PRO?**
₹299 per month or ₹2,388 per year, plus 18% GST — so ₹353 and ₹2,818 at checkout.

**Is the yearly plan cheaper?**
Yes. ₹2,388 a year works out well below twelve months at ₹299.

**Do I need PRO to use SVAR / Debug / Aptitude?**
No. All arenas are free on Basic. PRO gives more attempts per week and advanced reports.

**Do I need PRO to do an AI interview?**
No, but you need a credit. On Basic, earn one with 300 XP or buy a top-up pack.

**How do I get free interview credits?**
Every 300 XP converts to one interview credit, valid 3 months. See
`22-xp-levels-and-rewards.md`.

**Do unused credits roll over?**
The monthly PRO interview does **not** roll over. XP-earned credits last 3 months. Purchased
credits last for their pack's validity.

**Can I buy just one interview?**
Yes — the AI Interview – 1 pack, ₹99 before GST, valid one month.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled case by case by the support team. Do not state a refund policy — route the user
to support. See section 11.

**What payment methods work?**
Whatever Razorpay supports for your account — cards, UPI, netbanking.

**Where is my invoice?**
Payments page, in your order history — downloadable as a PDF.

**Does cancelling delete my account or my reports?**
No. You drop to Basic limits and keep your account, history and reports.

**Can I pause my subscription?**
No. You can cancel and resubscribe later.

**Is there a student discount?**
Not documented. Route to support rather than guessing.

## 10. What this does not do

- No plan other than Basic and PRO exists.
- Subscriptions cannot be paused.
- Downgrades never take effect immediately.
- Expired credits are not restored.
- **AlgoBuddy / counselling sessions are not currently available for purchase**, whatever the
  pricing page may still show.

## 11. When to contact support — and what never to state

**Always route to a human:**
- **Any refund request.** There is no published refund policy to quote. Do not state or imply one,
  in either direction — do not say refunds are available and do not say they are not. Say that
  refunds are reviewed by the support team and pass the request on.
- Money taken with nothing delivered.
- Double charges.
- A credit that has not returned more than an hour after a failed interview.
- Anything involving a chargeback or a dispute already opened with a bank.
- Questions about GST invoices for a company or institution.
