# XP, levels, leaderboards and rewards

## 1. What this is

Everything you do on AlgoJob earns **XP** (experience points). XP does two things: it moves you up
through six **levels**, each of which unlocks a reward, and — most usefully — it converts into
**free AI interview credits**. On the free plan, XP is how you get interviews.

## 2. Who can use it

Every candidate. PRO subscribers earn at higher daily caps and get a larger level-up bonus, but the
system works fully on Basic.

## 3. Names and terms

| A user might say | Meaning |
|---|---|
| points, coins, score | **XP** |
| rank, position | your place on a leaderboard |
| level, badge, tier | one of the six levels |
| streak | not a feature — see section 10 |
| credits | interview credits, which XP converts into — not XP itself |

⚠️ **XP is not a currency you spend.** You never spend XP. It accumulates, moves your level, and
automatically converts into interview credits at a fixed threshold.

## 4. How you earn XP

| Activity | XP | Daily cap (Basic) | Daily cap (PRO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing up | 25 | one-time | one-time |
| Completing your profile | 100 | one-time | one-time |
| Logging in | 5 | 5 XP (once a day) | 5 XP |
| Mock Aptitude | 1 per correct answer | 25 XP | **50 XP** |
| Coding practice | 2 per correct answer | 10 XP | **20 XP** |
| Mock SVAR | 20 per test | 20 XP (1 test) | 20 XP |
| Mock Debug | 20 per test | 20 XP (1 test) | 20 XP |
| AI Interview | 100 per interview | 1,000 XP (10 interviews) | 1,000 XP |

Remember the weekly arena limits still apply on top of these daily caps — 3 attempts a week on
Basic, 4 on PRO. See `00-platform-overview.md`.

## 5. XP to interview credits

**Every 300 XP earns you 1 AI interview credit, valid for 3 months.**

This conversion counts XP from logging in, Mock Aptitude, Mock SVAR, Mock Debug and AI interviews.
It happens automatically — there is no button to press.

On the Basic plan this is the only way to get interview credits without paying.

## 6. Levels

| Level | XP required | Level-up bonus XP |
|---|---|---|
| **Novice** | 0 | — |
| **Insignia** | 1,000 | 350 |
| **Oracle** | 2,500 | 700 |
| **Gauntlet** | 4,500 | 1,000 |
| **Mythos** | 7,000 | 1,300 |
| **Warlord** | 10,000 | 1,600 |

**PRO subscribers get a 1.5× multiplier on the level-up bonus.** Reaching Oracle on PRO pays 1,050
instead of 700.

Each level you reach can be **claimed**, which gives you a reward and a downloadable **certificate**
(PDF). Levels show as `locked`, `active` (reached, not yet claimed) or `claimed`.

Some level rewards are physical merchandise. Claiming one asks for a shipping address.

## 7. Leaderboards

- **Monthly leaderboard** — ranked on XP earned in the current month, so it resets each month and a
  new user can compete.
- **College leaderboard** — the same, limited to your college.
- **Skill rankings** — where you stand on individual skills.

Your all-time XP total is separate from your monthly leaderboard XP. Levels use your total; the
leaderboard uses the month.

## 8. When things go wrong

### "I finished a test but got no XP"
Scoring for SVAR, Debug and interviews happens in the background, and XP is awarded when scoring
finishes. If you left the page before that, the XP is granted the next time you visit. Open the
platform again and check your balance.

### "I hit my daily XP cap"
Caps are per activity per day — 25 XP a day from aptitude on Basic, 50 on PRO. Once capped, further
attempts that day still count against your weekly arena limit but stop earning XP. Come back
tomorrow.

### "My interview credit did not appear at 300 XP"
The conversion counts XP from logging in, aptitude, SVAR, Debug and interviews. XP from signing up
and completing your profile does not count towards it. Check the credit balance on the AI Interview
page rather than the XP total.

### "I went up a level but got no bonus"
The bonus is awarded at the moment you cross the threshold. If your level display has updated but
the bonus has not, refresh; if it still has not after that, contact support.

### "My leaderboard rank dropped even though I earned XP"
The monthly leaderboard ranks on XP earned this month. Others earning faster will pass you, and the
board resets at the start of each month.

### "My level says active but I cannot claim it"
Claiming a physical reward needs a shipping address. Add one under the reward claim flow.

### "My certificate will not download"
Certificates are generated as PDFs on claim. Try again, and if it still fails contact support with
the level name.

## 9. Common questions

**How do I get a free AI interview?**
Earn 300 XP. It converts automatically into one credit, valid 3 months.

**How fast can I earn 300 XP?**
Roughly: one SVAR and one Debug test is 40 XP, a full day of aptitude is up to 25 (50 on PRO), and
logging in is 5. Completing your profile is a one-off 100. A consistent week gets most people there.

**Do I lose XP if I do badly?**
No. XP is never deducted.

**Does XP expire?**
XP does not expire. The **interview credits** it converts into expire after 3 months.

**Can I buy XP?**
No. XP is earned only.

**Does PRO give me more XP per test?**
Not per test — the same 20 XP for a SVAR test, for example. PRO raises the daily **caps** and the
level-up bonus, and gives you an extra attempt per week.

**What is the highest level?**
Warlord, at 10,000 XP.

**Do my levels reset?**
No. Levels and total XP are permanent. Only the monthly leaderboard resets.

**Is there a daily streak bonus?**
There is a daily login reward of 5 XP. There is no separate streak multiplier.

## 10. What this does not do

- XP cannot be spent directly, transferred, or bought.
- XP is never deducted for poor performance.
- There is no streak multiplier beyond the daily login XP.
- **Referral rewards are not currently credited.** Referral codes exist, but do not promise a
  reward for referring someone — see `23-campus-buzz-and-profile.md`.
- The "Blue Tick" shown on the PRO plan card is **not currently available**.

## 11. When to contact support

- XP not awarded more than a day after a completed, scored test.
- A level crossed with no bonus and no certificate.
- A claimed physical reward with no shipping confirmation.
